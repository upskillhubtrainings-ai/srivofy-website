1. Who we are

This website, srivofy.com, is operated by Srivofy (“we”, “us”, “our”), an AI automation agency. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it and the choices you have. If you have any questions, contact us at inquiry@srivofy.com or +91-7207382886.

2. Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us:

  • Contact form: your name, business email, company (optional), the service you're interested in, your current challenge (optional) and your message.
  • Email, phone and WhatsApp: the details you share when you contact us directly.
  • Website chat assistant: the messages you type into the AI chat on this website. Please don't share sensitive personal information in the chat.

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when pages are requested — such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request — to keep the website secure and running. We do not use this to identify you.

We also collect usage information through Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel, as explained in “Cookies and tracking” below.

3. Cookies and tracking

We use two third-party tools that set cookies and collect information about your visit:

  • Google Analytics (Google) — helps us understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are viewed, how long visits last, the type of device and browser, approximate location (city/country) and how you arrived at the site.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta, the company behind Facebook and Instagram) — helps us measure the results of our advertising and show relevant ads to people who have visited our website.

These tools record page views and, when you send an enquiry, that an enquiry was made and which service it was about. They do not receive the contents of the contact form, such as your name, email or message. Google and Meta process this information under their own privacy policies.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on, and manage how Meta uses information for ads in your Meta ad preferences.

Some other features only load when you use them: demo videos are played from YouTube (in privacy-enhanced mode) when you press play, and WhatsApp links open WhatsApp. When you use these, those companies may collect information under their own privacy policies.

4. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a consultation or demo.
  • To answer your questions through the website chat assistant.
  • To prepare quotes and, if you become a client, to deliver our services.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it (Google Analytics).
  • To measure and improve our advertising (Meta Pixel).
  • To keep the website secure and prevent misuse, such as spam.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

5. Legal basis and consent

We process your personal data with your consent, which you give when you submit the contact form or contact us directly, and as needed to respond to your request, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing inquiry@srivofy.com.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business — such as our website hosting and email provider, and the automation and AI services that power the chat assistant — who may process it on our behalf, and only as needed to provide their services.

Website usage information is collected by Google (Google Analytics) and Meta (Meta Pixel) as described in “Cookies and tracking”.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and client records for as long as required for the engagement and our legal obligations. After that, we delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

8. How we protect it

We use reasonable security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limited access to enquiries. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly if a problem occurs.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it; or
  • stop using it (withdraw consent).

To make a request or raise a concern, email inquiry@srivofy.com. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. Please also read our Terms & Conditions.
