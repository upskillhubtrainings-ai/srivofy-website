About Srivofy

Practical AI. Thoughtful automation. Better workflows.

Srivofy is an AI automation agency. We help businesses identify where automation genuinely helps, design workflows around their people and systems, and build solutions that stay understandable and controllable.

Meet the founder

Srikanth, Founder of Srivofy

For 18 years I worked in IT as a Business Analyst and Project Manager. My job was understanding how a business really works, finding where things break, and making sure the right system got built.

With AI, there is now real scope to automate the repetitive work that slows small and medium businesses down: missed enquiries, slow replies, the same questions every day, no time for content.

I started Srivofy in 2025 to help business owners, in India and worldwide, put that automation in place the practical way: start with the real problem, map the workflow before building, and keep a person in charge of the steps that matter.

How we work

Four principles behind every project.

  1. 01

    Start with the problem

    We begin with the process that costs you time or customers — not with a tool looking for a use.

  2. 02

    Design the workflow

    Every automation is drawn step by step, with clear inputs, decisions and outcomes, before it is built.

  3. 03

    Connect your systems

    Automation is most useful when it works with the tools and data you already rely on.

  4. 04

    Keep people in control

    Human review, escalation and clear rules are part of the design, not an afterthought.

Our approach

From discovery to continuous improvement.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the business problem, process, and existing tools.

    • Map the current process and where time is lost
    • List the tools and data already in use
    • Agree what a good outcome looks like
  2. 02

    Design

    Define the workflow, AI capabilities, integrations, and human checkpoints.

    • Draw the target workflow step by step
    • Choose AI capabilities and integrations
    • Place human review where judgement matters
  3. 03

    Build

    Implement and test the agreed solution.

    • Configure AI, rules and connections
    • Test with realistic examples and edge cases
    • Prepare your team for go-live
  4. 04

    Optimize

    Review exceptions, evaluate results, and improve the workflow.

    • Review exceptions and handoffs
    • Compare results with the agreed outcome
    • Refine prompts, rules and routing

Our commitments

Honest about AI, from the first conversation.

  • We explain what AI will and will not do in your workflow.
  • We label demonstrations as demonstrations.
  • We agree data handling before anything goes live.
  • We measure results against goals agreed at the start.
