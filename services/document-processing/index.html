Document types

Invoices first — and the documents around them.

Invoice processing is the primary use case. The same pipeline can be extended to other supported business documents.

  • PDF invoices

    Text is read directly when the PDF contains it.

  • Scanned invoice PDFs

    OCR converts the scanned image into text.

  • Invoice images and photographs

    OCR for photos; quality affects results.

  • Receipts

    Merchant, date, totals and tax where present.

  • Purchase orders

    Order numbers, items and quantities.

  • Business forms

    Fields mapped to your own structure.

OCR may be required for scanned PDFs and images. Actual format support depends on the implementation and on document quality — low-resolution, skewed or handwritten files may need human review.

Workflow

The processing workflow

Seven steps from incoming file to a tracked, validated record.

  1. 01

    Receive PDF or Image

    Accept files through a configured upload, email, folder, API, or connected application.

  2. 02

    Extract Text

    Read embedded PDF text or use OCR for supported scanned files and images.

  3. 03

    Identify Invoice Fields

    Extract supplier information, dates, totals, and other configured fields.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Check required fields, data formats, totals, duplicate invoice numbers, and configured business rules.

  5. 05

    Review Exceptions

    Flag missing, ambiguous, or low-confidence fields for human review.

  6. 06

    Store in a Common Database

    Write validated records to the configured database or business system.

  7. 07

    Track Results

    Display success, failure, review-required, and duplicate statuses.

Data safety and accuracy

Built to catch problems, not hide them.

No extraction system is perfect. Ours is designed so uncertain data is checked before it is used. We do not claim certifications or compliance without evidence.

  • Configurable validation rules

    Required fields, formats, totals and your own business rules.

  • Duplicate checks

    Repeated invoice numbers or supplier/amount pairs are flagged.

  • Human review

    Uncertain or missing values go to a reviewer before they are stored.

  • Error handling

    Failed files are tracked with a reason, not silently dropped.

  • Access controls

    Who can view, review and export records is defined for the deployed system.

  • Retention and deletion

    How long files and records are kept is set by your agreed policy.

Security and data-handling requirements are agreed before any production deployment.
