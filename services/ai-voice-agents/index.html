Workflow

What happens during a call

Each call follows a defined path, ending with an action and a summary your team can use.

  1. 01

    Incoming Call

    The agent answers with your greeting.

  2. 02

    Speech Recognition

    The caller's speech is transcribed.

  3. 03

    AI Conversation

    The agent answers and asks the right questions.

  4. 04

    Action or Routing

    Book, route, escalate or create a request.

  5. 05

    Call Summary

    A structured summary is sent to your team.

Capabilities

Capabilities across every agent

  1. 01

    Natural conversation

    Callers speak normally; the agent responds from approved business information.

  2. 02

    Caller information capture

    Names, numbers and request details collected in a consistent format.

  3. 03

    Call routing

    Calls and requests directed to the right person or team by your rules.

  4. 04

    Human escalation

    Callers reach a person when a request is urgent, complex or sensitive.

  5. 05

    Structured call summaries

    Each call produces a short summary your team can act on.

  6. 06

    Follow-up triggers

    Start follow-up workflows, such as notifications or ticket creation, when configured.

Human handoff

People are always one step away.

You decide when a call should reach a person. These are typical rules we configure.

WhenThen
  • The caller asks for a personTransfer to the configured number or team.
  • The request is urgent or sensitiveEscalate immediately with the details collected so far.
  • The agent is unsure of an answerSay so honestly, capture the question and arrange a callback.
  • Outside business hoursTake a message and create a follow-up for the next working day.
