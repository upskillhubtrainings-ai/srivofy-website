Approved knowledge

It answers from what you approve — nothing else.

The assistant is configured with your business information. When a question falls outside it, the conversation goes to your team.

  • Services and pricing rules

    What you offer, how it is priced and what is included.

  • Policies

    Hours, locations, cancellations, delivery and payment terms.

  • Approved FAQs

    Answers your team has written or reviewed.

  • Escalation topics

    Subjects that always go straight to a person.
