## AI automation explained

### What is AI automation for business?

AI automation uses artificial intelligence to handle repetitive business work — answering calls and messages, capturing leads, drafting content or reading documents — and pass the results into the systems a team already uses. Good AI automation keeps people in control through approvals, review steps and human handoff.

### What is an AI voice agent?

An AI voice agent is software that answers phone calls in natural speech. It greets callers, answers approved questions, collects details such as name and reason for calling, takes appointment requests, routes or escalates calls to a person, and sends the team a short summary of each call.

### What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is an AI voice agent set up to do front-desk work: answering calls, sharing hours, location and service information, taking messages and appointment requests, and transferring callers to staff when needed — including outside business hours.

### What is a WhatsApp AI chatbot?

A WhatsApp AI chatbot (or WhatsApp AI agent) replies to customer messages on a WhatsApp Business number using information the business has approved. It answers common questions, captures and qualifies leads, collects appointment requests and hands the conversation to a person with the context already collected. It runs on the WhatsApp Business Platform.

### What is social media automation?

Social media automation uses AI to draft posts and captions from a brief, organise them in a content calendar, send them through review and approval, and schedule approved posts to the connected platforms, so a business can post consistently without a full-time content team.

### What is AI document processing?

AI document processing reads documents such as invoices, receipts and purchase orders — including scanned PDFs and photos, using OCR — extracts fields like supplier, date, amounts and line items, validates them, flags uncertain values for human review and saves approved records to a database or business system.

### What is invoice OCR?

Invoice OCR (optical character recognition) converts scanned or photographed invoices into text so that invoice fields can be extracted automatically. Text-based PDFs can be read directly; scanned files and images need OCR, and image quality affects accuracy.

### What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?

A basic chatbot follows fixed scripts and menus. An AI agent understands free-form questions, answers from approved business knowledge, asks follow-up questions and takes actions such as creating a lead, booking a request or handing off to a person.

### Does AI automation replace staff?

Srivofy designs AI automation to remove repetitive work, not people. The AI handles routine calls, messages and data entry, while staff review exceptions, approve important steps and handle complex or sensitive conversations.

### Is AI automation suitable for small businesses?

Yes. Small businesses often benefit most, because missed calls, slow WhatsApp replies and manual data entry cost them customers and time. A project can start with one workflow — such as answering calls or WhatsApp enquiries — and expand later.

## Document Processing

URL: https://srivofy.com/services/document-processing/

Srivofy helps businesses process invoices received as PDFs or images, extract the required information, validate the data, and store approved records in a common database.

### What it includes

- **Document intake:** Receive files by upload, email, shared folder, API or connected application.
- **Text extraction and OCR:** Read embedded PDF text, or use OCR for scanned PDFs and images.
- **Classification:** Identify invoices, receipts, purchase orders and other document types.
- **AI-assisted field extraction:** Locate supplier details, dates, amounts and line items.
- **Data normalization:** Standardise dates, currencies, number formats and supplier names.
- **Validation and duplicate checks:** Check required fields, totals and repeated invoice numbers.
- **Human review:** Route missing, ambiguous or low-confidence values to a reviewer.
- **Structured database entry:** Write approved records to your configured database or system.
- **Status and exception handling:** Track processed, review-required, duplicate and failed documents.

### How it works

1. **Receive PDF or Image:** Accept files through a configured upload, email, folder, API, or connected application.
2. **Extract Text:** Read embedded PDF text or use OCR for supported scanned files and images.
3. **Identify Invoice Fields:** Extract supplier information, dates, totals, and other configured fields.
4. **Validate:** Check required fields, data formats, totals, duplicate invoice numbers, and configured business rules.
5. **Review Exceptions:** Flag missing, ambiguous, or low-confidence fields for human review.
6. **Store in a Common Database:** Write validated records to the configured database or business system.
7. **Track Results:** Display success, failure, review-required, and duplicate statuses.

### Typical use cases

- **Accounts payable:** Supplier invoices captured into one table instead of scattered PDFs.
- **Expense receipts:** Photographed receipts turned into categorised expense records.
- **Purchase orders and forms:** Order and form details entered into business systems without retyping.

### How accurate is the extraction?

Accuracy depends on document quality, layout and the fields required. We test with your real samples, apply validation rules and route uncertain values to human review rather than claiming perfect accuracy.

### Can it read scanned invoices and photos?

Yes, using OCR, for supported image quality. Blurred, skewed or handwritten documents may need review.

### Which database can records go to?

Possible targets include PostgreSQL, MySQL, Supabase, Airtable, Google Sheets, CRM/ERP systems or a REST API. The destination is chosen and configured for your project.

### What happens to duplicates?

Configured duplicate checks — for example supplier plus invoice number — flag repeated invoices before they are stored.

### How is sensitive data handled?

Access controls, storage location, and retention and deletion policies are defined for the deployed system and agreed with you before go-live.
